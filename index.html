Every job that produced a video, grouped by arm and topic. Each card plays the rendered video beside a link to the Manim script that generated it. GitHub's file view cannot play these videos, which is why this page exists; the files themselves live under artifacts/, one folder per job, and the bulk archives are on the artifacts-v1 release.
